---
title: Snippet search and the menu bar
description: Find and insert any snippet with a shortcut, pin favorites to the menu bar, and pause expansion.
---

You can insert a snippet without typing its trigger, either from snippet search or from the menu bar.

## Snippet search

Press `⌥Space` anywhere to open snippet search. To change this shortcut, go to **Settings → Expansion** and use **Search snippets** under Shortcuts.

Type to search by trigger, label or content. Use `↑` and `↓` to move, `↩` to expand the selected snippet and `esc` to close. You can also click a snippet.

Snippet search only lists snippets that can expand in the app you are using. If none can, it says **No Snippets Here**, and you should check each group's app filter.

## The menu bar menu

Click the F Wrds icon in the menu bar.

- A status line at the top appears when something needs attention, for example **Expansion paused** or **Needs permission from macOS**.
- **Pinned** snippets sit at the top. To pin one, right-click it in the snippet list and choose **Pin to Menu Bar**. Choose **Unpin from Menu Bar** to remove it.
- **Recent** lists the snippets you expanded last.
- Choose a snippet in the menu to insert it.
- **Open F Wrds…** opens the manager window. The menu also has **Settings…**, **Help** and **Quit F Wrds**.

## Pause expansion

- Turn off **Expand snippets** to pause F Wrds everywhere.
- Turn on **Pause in** followed by the app name to pause only in the app you are using.
- To pause with a keyboard shortcut, set one in **Settings → Expansion**, under **Pause expansion**. It has no shortcut until you set one.

## Dock icon and launch at login

These are in **Settings → General**, under Startup.

- **Launch F Wrds at login** starts the app when you log in.
- **Open the F Wrds window at launch** opens the manager window each time.
- **Always show icon in the Dock** keeps the Dock icon visible. The Dock icon also shows while an F Wrds window is open.
