title: Snippets and triggers
description: Create snippets, set one or more triggers, use regex triggers, and control how case and word boundaries are handled.
---

A snippet is a trigger and the text it expands into. Snippets live in groups. See [Groups and app filters](/docs/groups).

## Add a snippet

1. Select a group in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add Snippet**.
3. Write what you want inserted in **Content**.
4. Optionally give it a **Label**. The label names the snippet in lists and in snippet search.

## Triggers

With **Text** selected under the trigger type, type a trigger such as `;sig` in **Add trigger** and click **Add**.

We suggest starting triggers with a semicolon. A leading `;` keeps a trigger from firing inside normal words. F Wrds does not require it.

A snippet can have several triggers, and any of them expands it. Click the cross on a trigger to remove it. A snippet with no trigger never expands, and F Wrds warns you about it. A trigger can be up to 64 characters long. A longer one is refused with a message, and so is a trigger the snippet already has.

## Regex triggers

Choose **Regex** to match a pattern instead of fixed text. Names captured by the pattern can be used in the content.

For example, with this pattern:

```text
:hi(?<name>\w+)
```

typing `:hiAnna` expands the snippet, and `{{name}}` in the content becomes `Anna`.

A snippet uses either Text triggers or a Regex, not both. Switch back to **Text** and your triggers are still there. An invalid pattern shows an error and the snippet won't expand until it is fixed.

## Options

- **Only expand after a space or punctuation**: the trigger only expands when it follows a space or a punctuation mark, so `sig` doesn't expand inside "design".
- **Match the case I type**: type the trigger with a capital letter and the expansion follows. Type it in capitals and the expansion is in capitals.
- **Capital letters**: how a capitalized trigger changes the text. Choose None, First letter, Each word or All capitals. Use it with **Match the case I type**.
- **Enabled**: turn a snippet off without deleting it.

## Undo an expansion

Press `⌫` as the very next key after an expansion and F Wrds puts back the trigger you typed. To turn this off, go to **Settings → Expansion** and clear **Press ⌫ right after an expansion to undo it**.
