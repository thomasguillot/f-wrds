title: Groups and app filters
description: Organize snippets into groups, reorder them, and choose which apps a group expands in.
---

Groups keep snippets together, like Work or Personal. Every snippet lives in a group.

## Work with groups

Click **Add Group** in the sidebar to make one. To rename a group, select it and press `↩`, or use the right-click menu and choose **Rename**.

- The checkbox next to a group turns all of its snippets on or off.
- Drag groups in the sidebar, and snippets in the list, to put them in order.
- Press `⌘⌫` to delete the selected group or snippet. You are asked to confirm first. Deleting a group also deletes its snippets, and this can't be undone.

To export a single group, see [Backup and import](/docs/backup/#export-to-espanso).

## Choose which apps a group works in

By default a group's snippets expand in every app. The button at the bottom of the snippet list shows where the group works:

- **All Apps**
- **Only in N apps**
- **Not in N apps**

Click the button to change it. It opens a sheet with two lists.

- **Only in these apps**: the group expands in these apps and nowhere else.
- **Never in these apps**: the group expands everywhere except in these apps.

Click **Add App…** under a list and pick an app. Leave both lists empty to expand everywhere.

## When a snippet does not expand

A snippet expands only when its group is enabled, the snippet is enabled, and the group's app filter includes the app you are in. If nothing expands, check these first. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/).
