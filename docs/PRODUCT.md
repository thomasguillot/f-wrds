# F Wrds: product context (marketing site)

## Register
Persuade. A one-page marketing site for a free, open source macOS menu-bar text expander.

## Users
Mac users who retype the same text daily (support, sales, writers, developers), and Espanso users who want a real management UI.

## Product
Type a trigger such as `;sig` or `:fwrds` in any app and it expands. Groups with app filters, variables with live preview, regex triggers, search picker, Espanso import/export, backup, in-app updates, English and French. Free, MIT, macOS 26+, unsigned, no account, no telemetry.

## Brand and tone
Cheeky, with a wink. Catchphrase: "#@!% typing the same $#!% twice." Grawlix only, never spelled-out profanity, with one symbol per letter of the root word and any ending kept: fuck is `#@!%`, shit is `$#!%`, fucking is `#@!%ing`. The `#!` in the logo is the mark, not a censored word. Plain, short sentences, US English, no em dashes, no hype words. Louder and more playful than the owner's other app sites.

## Anti-references
The owner's calmer app sites (Petit Cafe, Hosts Switchr); generic SaaS gradients and icon-card grids.

## Strategic principles
The expansion demo is the hero. Download and the unsigned-install note are always findable. Brand orange (#F06F15) to red (#D63A26) stays recognizable.
